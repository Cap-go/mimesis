How to play

No board, no cards to shuffle. Mimesis handles the teams, the timer, and the scores.

1

Make the teams

At least two teams of two players. Mimesis picks the next mime each turn.

2

Pick a theme

Idioms, rebus, art, unlikely situations… hundreds of cards to act out.

3

Mime without a word

No talking, singing, or writing. Each card your team finds scores a point.

Open source, built with Capgo
